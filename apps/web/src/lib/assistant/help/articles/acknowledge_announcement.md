Some announcements ask you to confirm you have read them. They show an **Action required** card on your dashboard and a count next to **Announcements** in the sidebar.

1. Open **Announcements** from the left sidebar (or click the item in the **Action required** card).
2. Select the **Needs my acknowledgement** tab.
3. Click **Read & acknowledge** on the announcement.
4. Read the message, tick **I have read and acknowledge this announcement**, and click **Acknowledge**.

Afterwards the announcement shows the date and time you acknowledged it. If the text is later changed significantly, you may be asked to acknowledge it again.
