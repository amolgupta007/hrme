Every announcement that requires acknowledgement has a status page showing exactly who has and hasn't confirmed it.

1. Open **Announcements** from the left sidebar.
2. On the announcement, click the **X of Y acknowledged** progress bar (or choose **Acknowledgement status** from its ⋯ menu).
3. Use the **Pending**, **Overdue**, **Acknowledged** and **Left** tabs, and the search box, to find people.
4. Click **Remind pending** to remind everyone outstanding, or **Remind** on a single row. Each person can be reminded at most once every 24 hours.
5. If people have joined the audience since publishing, click **Add them** on the banner to ask them to acknowledge too.
6. Click **Export CSV** to download the full list with acknowledgement times (IST).

Acknowledgement records cannot be edited or deleted. Deleting an announcement that has acknowledgements archives it instead, so the audit trail is kept. Employees who leave the organisation are shown under **Left** and don't count toward the total.
