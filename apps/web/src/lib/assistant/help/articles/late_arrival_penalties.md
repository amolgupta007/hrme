The **Late arrivals** tab shows every employee who was late in a month, what counted, and what action was taken.

1. Open **Attendance** from the left sidebar and select the **Late arrivals** tab.
2. Pick the **month** at the top right.
3. Click an employee to see each late day and the warnings or deductions applied.
4. To excuse a day that was marked late wrongly, click **Excuse**, enter a reason and click **Excuse day**. The month is recounted straight away, and a deduction that no longer applies is reversed automatically.
5. To cancel a deduction, click **Waive**, enter a reason and confirm. The leave is credited back, the loss of pay is removed from payroll, and the employee and their manager are emailed a correction.

A penalty marked **Needs review** changed after that month's payroll was already paid, so the loss of pay could not be adjusted automatically — waive it and correct the payout manually if needed. Every excuse and waiver is recorded with the admin's name and reason.
