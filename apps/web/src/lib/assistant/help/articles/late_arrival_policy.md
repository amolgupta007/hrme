A late-arrival policy counts each employee's late punch-ins in a calendar month and applies a consequence. An arrival is late when the punch-in is after the assigned shift's start time plus its grace minutes (or after the fallback cutoff when no shift is assigned). Week-offs, holidays, approved leave and days an admin has excused never count.

1. Open **Settings** from the left sidebar and go to **Attendance → Late Policy**.
2. Tick **Enabled** and give the rule a name.
3. Set a **Fallback cutoff** time for employees who have no shift assigned.
4. Choose a **Consequence**. **Warning + leave deduction** sends a formal warning at one late arrival (for example the 3rd) and deducts leave at another (for example the 5th, and every 5th after that).
5. For the warning + deduction rule, pick the **leave type**, **days deducted**, whether it **repeats**, and whether **loss of pay** applies when leave runs out. Check the **Preview** line.
6. Choose who is emailed: the employee always, with **reporting managers** and optionally **owners and admins** in CC.
7. Under **Applies to**, pick the departments and employees the policy covers.
8. Click **Save late policy**.

Late arrivals count from the day the policy is switched on — it never charges for earlier days. Leave is deducted first; if the balance runs out, the remainder becomes loss of pay in that month's payroll, shown on the payslip as "Late-arrival penalty".
