Announcements share news, policy updates and event notices with everyone or with specific departments and people.

1. Open **Announcements** from the left sidebar.
2. Click **New announcement**.
3. Enter a clear **Title** and choose a **Category**: General, Policy, Event, or Urgent.
4. Write the **Message**. Markdown is supported (**bold**, *italic*, headings and lists) — use **Preview** to check it.
5. Under **Audience**, keep **Everyone** (the default) or choose **Specific people** and pick departments and/or employees.
6. Toggle **Pin to top** if it should stay above other announcements.
7. Toggle **Require acknowledgement** if each recipient must confirm they have read it, and optionally set a **Due date**.
8. Click **Post announcement**.

With acknowledgement on, the audience is frozen at the moment you publish and each recipient is notified. People who join later are not added automatically — add them from the announcement's acknowledgement page. If you edit the text of an announcement people have already acknowledged, you choose whether they must acknowledge again or whether it was a minor fix.
