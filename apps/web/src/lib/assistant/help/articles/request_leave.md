You can submit a leave request for any leave type that your company has configured. Your manager and any org admins will be notified by email.

1. Open **Leave** from the left sidebar.
2. Click the **New Request** tab.
3. Choose the **Leave Type** (Paid, Sick, Casual, etc.) from the dropdown.
4. Pick your **Start Date** and **End Date** using the date pickers.
5. Add an optional **Reason** if you want to share context with your manager.
6. Click **Submit Request**.

Your request appears under the **My Requests** tab with a Pending status. You'll get an email when your manager approves or rejects it. The days are deducted from your balance only after approval.

**Comp-off.** If you worked on a week-off or a public holiday, pick your company's **Comp-off** leave type. Enter the **Day you worked**, the **Day off** you want in return, and a reason saying what you worked on. Comp-off has no balance: one worked day earns one day off (or a half day), and your manager approves it after checking that day's attendance. The day you worked must be a week-off or holiday for you, and each worked day can only be claimed once.

**Cancelling.** You can cancel a pending request, or an approved leave that hasn't started yet, from the **⋯** menu on the request. The days go back to your balance and your manager is emailed. Once an approved leave has started, only an admin can cancel it.
